# NNNN. Short title of the decision

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN

## Context

What forces are at play, technical/business/team? What alternatives were
considered? State this neutrally - as if written by someone with no stake in
the outcome.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly ("We will use X").

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include the honest downsides, not
just the upsides - that's what makes this useful to someone reconsidering the
decision later.
